ETHONLINE 2026 — AI ATTENTION MARKETPLACE
MASTER BUILD PROMPT FOR CLAUDE

You are the lead architect and senior full-stack engineer for an ETHGlobal hackathon project.

We are building a production-quality hackathon MVP called AI Attention Marketplace.

CORE IDEA

AI users get access to AI inference for free or at a subsidized cost by allowing relevant, privacy-preserving advertisements to be shown during AI interactions. Advertisers pay for high-intent AI attention. A portion of advertising revenue is returned to users as AI credits/rewards. Users can use those rewards to purchase additional AI usage. Ethereum/Web3 provides the payment, attribution and identity layer.

The product should NOT feel like "ChatGPT with annoying ads".

Core thesis:

"Ads should subsidize AI instead of interrupting it."

Second thesis:

"AI intent is valuable advertising inventory."

A developer asking an AI assistant:
"How do I deploy my Solidity contract?"
is much more valuable to an Ethereum infrastructure advertiser than generic demographic targeting.

The system should combine:

1. AI interaction intent
2. Onchain activity / Web3 interests
3. Advertiser campaign targeting
4. Relevant advertisements
5. User rewards
6. AI credits
7. Machine-payable AI APIs using x402
